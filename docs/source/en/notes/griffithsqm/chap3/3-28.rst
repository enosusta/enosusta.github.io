Problem 3.28
==================

a.  From the power series for the sine function,

    .. math::
        \sin\hat{D}
        =\hat{D}-\frac{\hat{D}^{\,3}}{3!}
        +\frac{\hat{D}^{\,5}}{5!}
        -\frac{\hat{D}^{\,7}}{7!}+\cdots.

    When this operator acts on `x^5`, all derivatives of order six or higher vanish. Also,

    .. math::
        \hat{D}x^5=5x^4,
        \quad
        \hat{D}^{\,3}x^5=60x^2,
        \quad
        \hat{D}^{\,5}x^5=120,

    so

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \left(\sin\hat{D}\right)x^5
            &=5x^4-\frac{60x^2}{3!}+\frac{120}{5!}
            \\
            &=5x^4-10x^2+1.
        \end{aligned}

b.  Using the geometric series,

    .. math::
        \frac{1}{\mathbf{1}-\hat{D}/2}
        =\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}
        \left(\frac{\hat{D}}{2}\right)^n.

    The derivatives of `\cos x` satisfy

    .. math::
        \hat{D}^{\,2k}\cos x=(-1)^k\cos x,
        \quad
        \hat{D}^{\,2k+1}\cos x=(-1)^{k+1}\sin x.

    Separating the even- and odd-order terms gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \left(
                \frac{1}{\mathbf{1}-\hat{D}/2}
            \right)\cos x
            &=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
            \frac{(-1)^k}{2^{2k}}\cos x
            +\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
            \frac{(-1)^{k+1}}{2^{2k+1}}\sin x
            \\
            &=\frac{1}{1+1/4}\cos x
            -\frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{1+1/4}\sin x
            \\
            &=\frac{4}{5}\cos x-\frac{2}{5}\sin x.
        \end{aligned}

Problem 3.44
==================

a.  The vector

    .. math::
        |c\rangle
        =\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}

    is an eigenvector with eigenvalue `c`. In the subspace spanned by the first and third components, it is enough to diagonalize

    .. math::
        \begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&a\end{pmatrix}.

    Its normalized eigenvectors and eigenvalues are

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            |+\rangle
            &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
            \begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix},
            \quad E_+=a+b
            \\
            |-\rangle
            &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
            \begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\-1\end{pmatrix},
            \quad E_-=a-b.
        \end{aligned}

    Since the initial state is `|c\rangle` itself, it acquires only an overall phase under time evolution:

    .. math::
        |S(t)\rangle
        =e^{-ict/\hbar}
        \begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}.

b.  In this case, the initial state can be expanded as

    .. math::
        \begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
        =\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \left(|+\rangle+|-\rangle\right).

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            |S(t)\rangle
            &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
            \left[
                e^{-i\left(a+b\right)t/\hbar}|+\rangle
                +e^{-i\left(a-b\right)t/\hbar}|-\rangle
            \right]
            \\
            &=\frac{1}{2}
            \begin{pmatrix}
                e^{-i\left(a+b\right)t/\hbar}
                +e^{-i\left(a-b\right)t/\hbar}
                \\
                0
                \\
                e^{-i\left(a+b\right)t/\hbar}
                -e^{-i\left(a-b\right)t/\hbar}
            \end{pmatrix}
            \\
            &=e^{-iat/\hbar}
            \begin{pmatrix}
                \cos\left(bt/\hbar\right)
                \\
                0
                \\
                -i\sin\left(bt/\hbar\right)
            \end{pmatrix}.
        \end{aligned}

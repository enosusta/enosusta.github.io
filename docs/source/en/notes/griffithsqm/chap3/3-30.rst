Problem 3.30
==================

Let the position-space wave function be

.. math::
    \Psi(x,t)=\braket{x|S(t)}.

Its components in the energy basis are

.. math::
    c_n(t)=\braket{n|S(t)}.

Inserting the identity operator in the position basis into this inner product gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        c_n(t)
        &=\left\langle
            n\middle|
            \left(
                \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
                |x\rangle\langle x|\,\dd x
            \right)
            \middle|S(t)
        \right\rangle
        \\
        &=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
        \braket{n|x}\braket{x|S(t)}\,\dd x.
    \end{aligned}

Writing the `n`-th energy eigenstate in the position representation as

.. math::
    \psi_n(x)=\braket{x|n},

we have

.. math::
    \braket{n|x}=\braket{x|n}^*=\psi_n(x)^*,

and therefore

.. math::
    c_n(t)
    =\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
    \psi_n(x)^*\Psi(x,t)\,\dd x.

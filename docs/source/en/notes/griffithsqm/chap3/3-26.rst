Problem 3.26
==================

a.  Complex conjugating the coefficients of the kets gives

    .. math::
        \langle\alpha|
        =-i\langle1|-2\langle2|+i\langle3|,
        \quad
        \langle\beta|
        =-i\langle1|+2\langle3|.

    In component form,

    .. math::
        \langle\alpha|
        =\begin{pmatrix}-i&-2&i\end{pmatrix},
        \quad
        \langle\beta|
        =\begin{pmatrix}-i&0&2\end{pmatrix}.

b.  By orthonormality,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{\alpha|\beta}
            &=(-i)i+(-2)0+i(2)
            =1+2i
            \\
            \braket{\beta|\alpha}
            &=(-i)i+0(-2)+2(-i)
            =1-2i.
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \braket{\beta|\alpha}
        =1-2i
        =\left(1+2i\right)^*
        =\braket{\alpha|\beta}^*.

c.  The matrix of the outer product `\hat{A}=|\alpha\rangle\langle\beta|` is

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \mathbf{A}
            &=\begin{pmatrix}i\\-2\\-i\end{pmatrix}
            \begin{pmatrix}-i&0&2\end{pmatrix}
            \\
            &=\begin{pmatrix}
                1 & 0 & 2i
                \\
                2i & 0 & -4
                \\
                -1 & 0 & -2i
            \end{pmatrix}.
        \end{aligned}

    This matrix is clearly not Hermitian.

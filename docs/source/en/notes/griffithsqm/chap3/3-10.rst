Problem 3.10
==================

Taking the infinite square well to occupy `0<x<a`, the normalized ground state is

.. math::
    \psi_1(x)=\sqrt{\frac{2}{a}}
    \sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right)
    \quad (0<x<a),

and it is zero outside the well. Applying the momentum operator inside the well gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \hat{p}\psi_1
        &=-i\hbar\dv{\psi_1}{x}
        \\
        &=-i\hbar\sqrt{\frac{2}{a}}\frac{\pi}{a}
        \cos\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right),
    \end{aligned}

so this state does not have a definite momentum.

The momentum eigenvalue equation

.. math::
    -i\hbar\dv{\psi}{x}=p\psi

has the nontrivial solution `\psi(x)=Ce^{ipx/\hbar}`. This function cannot satisfy the infinite-square-well boundary conditions `\psi(0)=\psi(a)=0`.

Problem 3.37
==================

Since `\hat{Q}=xp` has no explicit time dependence, the generalized Ehrenfest theorem gives

.. math::
    \dv{}{t}\braket{xp}
    =\frac{i}{\hbar}\braket{[\hat{H},xp]}.

Here,

.. math::
    \hat{H}=\frac{p^2}{2m}+V(x),
    \quad
    [x,p]=i\hbar.

First,

.. math::
    [p^2,x]
    =p[p,x]+[p,x]p
    =-2i\hbar p,

and hence

.. math::
    [p^2,xp]
    =[p^2,x]p+x[p^2,p]
    =-2i\hbar p^2.

Also, since `[V(x),x]=0` and `[V(x),p]=i\hbar V'(x)`,

.. math::
    [V(x),xp]
    =[V(x),x]p+x[V(x),p]
    =i\hbar xV'(x).

Therefore,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \dv{}{t}\braket{xp}
        &=\frac{i}{\hbar}
        \left\langle
            -\frac{i\hbar}{m}p^2
            +i\hbar xV'(x)
        \right\rangle
        \\
        &=\frac{\braket{p^2}}{m}
        -\braket{xV'(x)}
        \\
        &=2\braket{T}
        -\left\langle x\pdv{V}{x}\right\rangle.
    \end{aligned}

In a stationary state,

.. math::
    |\Psi(t)\rangle
    =e^{-iEt/\hbar}|\psi\rangle,

and for any operator `\hat{Q}` with no explicit time dependence,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{\Psi(t)|\hat{Q}|\Psi(t)}
        &=e^{iEt/\hbar}e^{-iEt/\hbar}
        \braket{\psi|\hat{Q}|\psi}
        \\
        &=\braket{\psi|\hat{Q}|\psi}.
    \end{aligned}

Thus, `\braket{xp}` is also independent of time, and therefore

.. math::
    2\braket{T}
    =\left\langle x\dv{V}{x}\right\rangle.

For the harmonic oscillator,

.. math::
    V(x)=\frac{1}{2}m\omega^2x^2,
    \quad
    xV'(x)=m\omega^2x^2=2V(x),

so the virial theorem gives

.. math::
    2\braket{T}=2\braket{V},
    \quad
    \braket{T}=\braket{V}.

Also, the total energy of a stationary state is

.. math::
    E_n=\left(n+\frac{1}{2}\right)\hbar\omega
    =\braket{T}_n+\braket{V}_n,

and hence

.. math::
    \braket{T}_n=\braket{V}_n
    =\frac{1}{2}\left(n+\frac{1}{2}\right)\hbar\omega.

This agrees with the result of :ref:`notes/griffithsqm/chap2/2-12:Problem 2.12`.

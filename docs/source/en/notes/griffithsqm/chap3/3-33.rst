Problem 3.33
==================

a.  Immediately after measuring `a_1` for the observable `A`, the state collapses to the corresponding normalized eigenstate, so

    .. math::
        |\Psi\rangle=|\psi_1\rangle,

    up to an arbitrary phase factor.

b.  Expressing the state immediately before the measurement in the eigenbasis of `B` gives

    .. math::
        |\psi_1\rangle
        =\frac{3}{5}|\phi_1\rangle
        +\frac{4}{5}|\phi_2\rangle.

    Therefore, the possible measurement outcomes and their probabilities according to the Born rule are

    .. math::
        P(b_1)=\frac{9}{25},
        \quad
        P(b_2)=\frac{16}{25}.

c.  The inverse of the given change of basis is

    .. math::
        |\phi_1\rangle
        =\frac{3}{5}|\psi_1\rangle
        +\frac{4}{5}|\psi_2\rangle,
        \quad
        |\phi_2\rangle
        =\frac{4}{5}|\psi_1\rangle
        -\frac{3}{5}|\psi_2\rangle.

    Therefore, the conditional probabilities are

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            P(a_1 | b_1)
            &=|\braket{\psi_1|\phi_1}|^2
            =\frac{9}{25}
            \\
            P(a_1 | b_2)
            &=|\braket{\psi_1|\phi_2}|^2
            =\frac{16}{25}.
        \end{aligned}

    Since the result of the measurement of `B` is not known,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            P(a_1)
            &=P(b_1)P(a_1 | b_1)
            +P(b_2)P(a_1 | b_2)
            \\
            &=\frac{9}{25}\frac{9}{25}
            +\frac{16}{25}\frac{16}{25}
            \\
            &=\frac{337}{625}.
        \end{aligned}

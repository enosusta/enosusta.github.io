Problem 3.5
==================

a.  Since `x` is real,

    .. math::
        \braket{f|xg}
        =\int f^*xg\,\dd x
        =\int(xf)^*g\,\dd x
        =\braket{xf|g}.

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        x^\dagger=x.

    For the constant `i`,

    .. math::
        \braket{f|ig}
        =i\braket{f|g}
        =\braket{-if|g},

    so

    .. math::
        i^\dagger=-i.

    Finally, integration by parts gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{f|\dv{}{x}g}
            &=\int f^*g'\,\dd x
            \\
            &=\left[f^*g\right]_{\text{boundary}}
            -\int f'^*g\,\dd x
            \\
            &=\braket{-\dv{}{x}f|g},
        \end{aligned}

    and hence

    .. math::
        \left(\dv{}{x}\right)^\dagger=-\dv{}{x}.

b.  By definition,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{f|\hat{Q}\hat{R}g}
            &=\braket{\hat{Q}^\dagger f|\hat{R}g}
            \\
            &=\braket{\hat{R}^\dagger\hat{Q}^\dagger f|g},
        \end{aligned}

    so

    .. math::
        (\hat{Q}\hat{R})^\dagger
        =\hat{R}^\dagger\hat{Q}^\dagger.

    Similarly,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{f|(\hat{Q}+\hat{R})g}
            &=\braket{\hat{Q}^\dagger f|g}
            +\braket{\hat{R}^\dagger f|g}
            \\
            &=\braket{(\hat{Q}^\dagger+\hat{R}^\dagger)f|g},
        \end{aligned}

    and hence

    .. math::
        (\hat{Q}+\hat{R})^\dagger
        =\hat{Q}^\dagger+\hat{R}^\dagger.

    Also,

    .. math::
        \braket{f|c\hat{Q}g}
        =c\braket{\hat{Q}^\dagger f|g}
        =\braket{c^*\hat{Q}^\dagger f|g},

    so

    .. math::
        (c\hat{Q})^\dagger
        =c^*\hat{Q}^\dagger.

c.  Equation (2.48) gives

    .. math::
        \hat{a}_+
        =\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\hbar m\omega}}
        \left(-i\hat{p}+m\omega x\right),

    and therefore

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \hat{a}_+^\dagger
            &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\hbar m\omega}}
            \left[(-i\hat{p})^\dagger+(m\omega x)^\dagger\right]
            \\
            &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\hbar m\omega}}
            \left(i\hat{p}+m\omega x\right)
            \\
            &=\hat{a}_-.
        \end{aligned}

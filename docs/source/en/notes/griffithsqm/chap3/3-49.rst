Problem 3.49
==================

a.  The Schrödinger equation for a free particle in the position representation is

    .. math::
        i\hbar\pdv{\Psi}{t}
        =-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\pdv{^2\Psi}{x^2}.

    Under the Fourier transform

    .. math::
        \Phi(p,t)
        =\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}
        \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
        e^{-ipx/\hbar}\Psi(x,t)\,\dd x,

    the operator `-i\hbar\partial/\partial x` becomes multiplication by `p`. Therefore, the equation in momentum space is

    .. math::
        i\hbar\pdv{\Phi(p,t)}{t}
        =\frac{p^2}{2m}\Phi(p,t).

    This is an independent first-order ordinary differential equation for each `p`. Integrating

    .. math::
        \frac{1}{\Phi}\pdv{\Phi}{t}
        =-\frac{ip^2}{2m\hbar}

    gives

    .. math::
        \Phi(p,t)
        =e^{-ip^2t/(2m\hbar)}\Phi(p,0).

b.  The initial state in :ref:`notes/griffithsqm/chap2/2-42:Problem 2.42` is

    .. math::
        \Psi(x,0)=Ae^{-ax^2}e^{il x},
        \quad
        A=\left(\frac{2a}{\pi}\right)^{1/4},

    and its Fourier transform is

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \Phi(p,0)
            &=\frac{A}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}
            \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
            \exp\left[
                -ax^2-i\left(\frac{p}{\hbar}-l\right)x
            \right]\,\dd x
            \\
            &=\frac{A}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}
            \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{a}}
            \exp\left[
                -\frac{\left(p/\hbar-l\right)^2}{4a}
            \right]
            \\
            &=\frac{1}{\left(2\pi a\hbar^2\right)^{1/4}}
            \exp\left[
                -\frac{\left(p-\hbar l\right)^2}
                {4a\hbar^2}
            \right].
        \end{aligned}

    Multiplying by the time-evolution factor from \(a) gives

    .. math::
        \Phi(p,t)
        =\frac{1}{\left(2\pi a\hbar^2\right)^{1/4}}
        \exp\left[
            -\frac{\left(p-\hbar l\right)^2}{4a\hbar^2}
            -\frac{ip^2t}{2m\hbar}
        \right].

    The probability density is

    .. math::
        |\Phi(p,t)|^2
        =\frac{1}{\hbar\sqrt{2\pi a}}
        \exp\left[
            -\frac{\left(p-\hbar l\right)^2}{2a\hbar^2}
        \right],

    which is independent of time.

c.  For convenience, define

    .. math::
        p_0\coloneqq\hbar l,
        \quad
        s^2\coloneqq a\hbar^2,
        \quad
        q=p-p_0.

    The probability density is then the Gaussian distribution

    .. math::
        |\Phi|^2
        =\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}s}
        e^{-q^2/(2s^2)}.

    The integrals of odd powers of `q` vanish, and

    .. math::
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}s}
        \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
        q^2e^{-q^2/(2s^2)}\,\dd q
        =s^2.

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{p}
            &=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
            \left(p_0+q\right)|\Phi|^2\,\dd q
            =p_0
            \\
            \braket{p^2}
            &=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
            \left(p_0+q\right)^2|\Phi|^2\,\dd q
            =p_0^2+s^2.
        \end{aligned}

    Hence,

    .. math::
        \braket{p}=\hbar l,
        \quad
        \braket{p^2}=\hbar^2l^2+a\hbar^2,
        \quad
        \sigma_p^2=a\hbar^2.

    This agrees with the result of :ref:`notes/griffithsqm/chap2/2-42:Problem 2.42`.

d.  For a free particle, `H=p^2/(2m)`, so by \(c),

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{H}
            &=\frac{\braket{p^2}}{2m}
            \\
            &=\frac{\hbar^2l^2}{2m}
            +\frac{a\hbar^2}{2m}
            \\
            &=\frac{\braket{p}^2}{2m}+\braket{H}_0.
        \end{aligned}

    Here,

    .. math::
        \braket{H}_0=\frac{a\hbar^2}{2m}

    is the mean energy of the Gaussian wave packet with `l=0` and zero mean momentum. The first term is the classical kinetic energy associated with the mean momentum of the wave packet as a whole, that is, with the motion of its center. The second term is the kinetic energy that remains because the wave packet is localized to a finite width. Thus, even when the mean momentum is zero, the mean energy of the wave packet is nonzero, and the packet spreads with time.

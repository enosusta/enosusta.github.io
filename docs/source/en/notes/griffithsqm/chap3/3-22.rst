Problem 3.22
==================

Choosing `Q=x` as the observable, the energy-time uncertainty principle becomes

.. math::
    \sigma_H\sigma_x
    \geq
    \frac{\hbar}{2}
    \left|\dv{\braket{x}}{t}\right|.

On the other hand, from \(c) of :ref:`notes/griffithsqm/chap3/3-18:Problem 3.18`,

.. math::
    \dv{\braket{x}}{t}=\frac{\braket{p}}{m},

and therefore

.. math::
    \sigma_x\sigma_H
    \geq
    \frac{\hbar}{2m}|\braket{p}|.

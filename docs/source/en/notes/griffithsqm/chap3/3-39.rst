Problem 3.39
==================

For convenience, define

.. math::
    c=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}},
    \quad
    d=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar m\omega}{2}}.

The position and momentum operators can then be written in terms of the raising and lowering operators as

.. math::
    x=c\left(a_++a_-\right),
    \quad
    p=id\left(a_+-a_-\right).

Also,

.. math::
    a_+|n'\rangle
    =\sqrt{n'+1}|n'+1\rangle,
    \quad
    a_-|n'\rangle
    =\sqrt{n'}|n'-1\rangle,

so the matrix elements of position are

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{n|x|n'}
        &=c\left(
            \sqrt{n'+1}\,\delta_{n,n'+1}
            +\sqrt{n'}\,\delta_{n,n'-1}
        \right)
        \\
        &=c\left(
            \sqrt{n}\,\delta_{n',n-1}
            +\sqrt{n'}\,\delta_{n,n'-1}
        \right).
    \end{aligned}

Similarly, the matrix elements of momentum are

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{n|p|n'}
        &=id\left(
            \sqrt{n'+1}\,\delta_{n,n'+1}
            -\sqrt{n'}\,\delta_{n,n'-1}
        \right)
        \\
        &=id\left(
            \sqrt{n}\,\delta_{n',n-1}
            -\sqrt{n'}\,\delta_{n,n'-1}
        \right).
    \end{aligned}

Thus, the corresponding infinite matrices are

.. math::
    \bm{X}
    =c\begin{pmatrix}
        0 & 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots
        \\
        1 & 0 & \sqrt{2} & 0 & \cdots
        \\
        0 & \sqrt{2} & 0 & \sqrt{3} & \cdots
        \\
        0 & 0 & \sqrt{3} & 0 & \cdots
        \\
        \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots
    \end{pmatrix}

and

.. math::
    \bm{P}
    =id\begin{pmatrix}
        0 & -1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots
        \\
        1 & 0 & -\sqrt{2} & 0 & \cdots
        \\
        0 & \sqrt{2} & 0 & -\sqrt{3} & \cdots
        \\
        0 & 0 & \sqrt{3} & 0 & \cdots
        \\
        \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots
    \end{pmatrix},

respectively. On the other hand, since `c^2=\hbar/(2m\omega)` and `d^2=\hbar m\omega/2`,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \frac{p^2}{2m}
        &=-\frac{\hbar\omega}{4}
        \left(
            a_+^2-a_+a_--a_-a_++a_-^2
        \right)
        \\
        \frac{m\omega^2x^2}{2}
        &=\frac{\hbar\omega}{4}
        \left(
            a_+^2+a_+a_-+a_-a_++a_-^2
        \right).
    \end{aligned}

Adding these expressions gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        H
        &=\frac{\hbar\omega}{2}
        \left(a_+a_-+a_-a_+\right)
        \\
        &=\hbar\omega
        \left(a_+a_-+\frac{1}{2}\right).
    \end{aligned}

Since `a_+a_-|n\rangle=n|n\rangle`,

.. math::
    \braket{n|H|n'}
    =\hbar\omega
    \left(n+\frac{1}{2}\right)\delta_{nn'}.

Therefore,

.. math::
    \frac{1}{2m}\mathbf{P}^2
    +\frac{m\omega^2}{2}\mathbf{X}^2
    =\mathbf{H}
    =\hbar\omega
    \begin{pmatrix}
        \dfrac{1}{2} & 0 & 0 & \cdots
        \\
        0 & \dfrac{3}{2} & 0 & \cdots
        \\
        0 & 0 & \dfrac{5}{2} & \cdots
        \\
        \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots
    \end{pmatrix}.

As expected, the diagonal elements are `E_n=(n+1/2)\hbar\omega`.

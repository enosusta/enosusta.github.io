Problem 3.19
==================

a.  From :ref:`notes/griffithsqm/chap3/3-18:Problem 3.18`,

    .. math::
        \dv{\braket{x}}{t}
        =\frac{\braket{p}}{m},
        \quad
        \dv{\braket{p}}{t}
        =-\left\langle\dv{V}{x}\right\rangle.

    For a free particle, `V(x)=0`, so

    .. math::
        \dv{\braket{p}}{t}=0.

    Therefore, `\braket{p}` is independent of time. Substituting this result into the first equation and integrating gives

    .. math::
        \braket{x}(t)
        =\braket{x}(0)
        +\frac{\braket{p}}{m}t.

b.  For the harmonic oscillator,

    .. math::
        V(x)=\frac{1}{2}m\omega^2x^2,
        \quad
        \dv{V}{x}=m\omega^2x,

    and hence

    .. math::
        \dv{\braket{p}}{t}
        =-m\omega^2\braket{x}.

    On the other hand, differentiating `\dd\braket{x}/\dd t=\braket{p}/m` once more gives

    .. math::
        \dv{^2\braket{x}}{t^2}
        =\frac{1}{m}\dv{\braket{p}}{t}
        =-\omega^2\braket{x}.

    Therefore, `\braket{x}` satisfies the classical simple-harmonic-motion equation.

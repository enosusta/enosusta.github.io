Problem 3.32
==================

a.  Let the expectation value in the state `|\psi\rangle` be

    .. math::
        q=\braket{\psi|\hat{Q}\psi}.

    From the definition of the adjoint and `\hat{Q}^\dagger=-\hat{Q}`,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            q
            &=\braket{\hat{Q}^\dagger\psi|\psi}
            \\
            &=\braket{-\hat{Q}\psi|\psi}
            \\
            &=-\braket{\hat{Q}\psi|\psi}
            =-q^*.
        \end{aligned}

    Hence, `\operatorname{Re}q=0`.

b.  Let `\hat{Q}|q\rangle=q|q\rangle`, where `|q\rangle\neq0`. Then

    .. math::
        \braket{q|\hat{Q}|q}
        =q\braket{q|q}.

    On the other hand,

    .. math::
        \hat{Q}^\dagger|q\rangle
        =-\hat{Q}|q\rangle
        =-q|q\rangle,

    so

    .. math::
        \braket{q|\hat{Q}|q}
        =\braket{\hat{Q}^\dagger q|q}
        =-q^*\braket{q|q}.

    Since `\braket{q|q}>0`, we have `q=-q^*`.

c.  Consider the two eigenvalue equations

    .. math::
        \hat{Q}|q\rangle=q|q\rangle,
        \quad
        \hat{Q}|q'\rangle=q'|q'\rangle.

    Then

    .. math::
        \braket{q|\hat{Q}|q'}
        =q'\braket{q|q'}.

    On the other hand, using `q^*=-q` from \(b),

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{q|\hat{Q}|q'}
            &=\braket{\hat{Q}^\dagger q|q'}
            \\
            &=\braket{-\hat{Q}q|q'}
            \\
            &=-q^*\braket{q|q'}
            =q\braket{q|q'}.
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \left(q'-q\right)\braket{q|q'}=0,

    and if `q'\neq q`, then `\braket{q|q'}=0`.

d.  If `\hat{A}` and `\hat{B}` are both Hermitian, then

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            [\hat{A},\hat{B}]^\dagger
            &=\left(\hat{A}\hat{B}-\hat{B}\hat{A}\right)^\dagger
            \\
            &=\hat{B}^\dagger\hat{A}^\dagger
            -\hat{A}^\dagger\hat{B}^\dagger
            \\
            &=\hat{B}\hat{A}-\hat{A}\hat{B}
            =-[\hat{A},\hat{B}],
        \end{aligned}

    so their commutator is anti-Hermitian. If `\hat{A}` and `\hat{B}` are both anti-Hermitian, then likewise

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            [\hat{A},\hat{B}]^\dagger
            &=\left(-\hat{B}\right)\left(-\hat{A}\right)
            -\left(-\hat{A}\right)\left(-\hat{B}\right)
            \\
            &=\hat{B}\hat{A}-\hat{A}\hat{B}
            =-[\hat{A},\hat{B}],
        \end{aligned}

    so their commutator is also anti-Hermitian.

e.  Define the following two operators:

    .. math::
        \hat{A}
        =\frac{\hat{Q}+\hat{Q}^\dagger}{2},
        \quad
        \hat{B}
        =\frac{\hat{Q}-\hat{Q}^\dagger}{2}.

    Then `\hat{Q}=\hat{A}+\hat{B}`, and

    .. math::
        \hat{A}^\dagger
        =\frac{\hat{Q}^\dagger+\hat{Q}}{2}
        =\hat{A},
        \quad
        \hat{B}^\dagger
        =\frac{\hat{Q}^\dagger-\hat{Q}}{2}
        =-\hat{B}.

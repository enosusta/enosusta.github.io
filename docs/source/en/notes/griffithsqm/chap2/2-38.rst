Problem 2.38
==================

a.  At the quantum recurrence time, the time dependence `e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}` of the wave function satisfies

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \exp\left(-i\frac{E_nT}{\hbar}\right) &= \exp\left(-i\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2ma^2}\frac{1}{\hbar}\frac{4ma^2}{\pi\hbar}\right)
            \\
            &= \exp(-i2\pi n^2) = 1,
        \end{aligned}

    so

    .. math::
        \Psi(x,T) = \Psi(x,0).

b.  The kinetic energy of a classical particle is

    .. math::
        E = \frac{1}{2}mv^2,

    so its speed is `v=\sqrt{2E/m}`. Therefore, the classical recurrence time is

    .. math::
        T_\text{cl} = \frac{2a}{v} = a\sqrt{\frac{2m}{E}}.

c.  When the two recurrence times are equal,

    .. math::
        \frac{4ma^2}{\pi\hbar} = a\sqrt{\frac{2m}{E}},

    and hence the energy is

    .. math::
        E = \frac{\pi^2\hbar^2}{8ma^2}.

    However, this does not coincide with the energy of any actually allowed stationary state.

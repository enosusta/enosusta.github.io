Problem 2.34
==================

a.  The wave function is

    .. math::
        \psi(x) = \begin{cases}
            Ae^{ikx} + Be^{-ikx} & (x<0)
            \\
            Fe^{-\kappa x} & (x>0).
        \end{cases}

    Continuity of `\psi` and `\psi'` at `x=0` gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            A + B &= F
            \\
            ik(A-B) &= -\kappa F,
        \end{aligned}

    so

    .. math::
        (ik+\kappa)A = (ik-\kappa)B,

    and hence

    .. math::
        \frac{B}{A} = \frac{ik+\kappa}{ik-\kappa} = \frac{k-i\kappa}{k+i\kappa}.

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        R = \frac{k^2+\kappa^2}{k^2+\kappa^2} = 1.

    Thus, there is no transmission.

b.  The wave function is

    .. math::
        \psi(x) = \begin{cases}
            Ae^{ikx} + Be^{-ikx} & (x<0)
            \\
            Fe^{iqx} & (x>0),
        \end{cases}

    where

    .. math::
        q = \frac{\sqrt{2m(E-V_0)}}{\hbar}.

    Continuity of `\psi` and `\psi'` at `x=0` gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            A + B &= F
            \\
            k(A-B) &= qF,
        \end{aligned}

    so

    .. math::
        (k-q)A = (k+q)B,

    and hence

    .. math::
        \frac{B}{A} = \frac{k-q}{k+q}.

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        R = \left(\frac{k-q}{k+q}\right)^2.

c.  In general, the probability current of a plane wave `\psi = Ce^{isx}` is

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            J &= \frac{\hbar}{2mi}\left(\psi^*\dv{\psi}{x}-\psi\dv{\psi^*}{x}\right)
            \\
            &= \frac{\hbar s}{m}|C|^2.
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        J_\text{inc} = \frac{\hbar k}{m}|A|^2, \quad J_\text{trans} = \frac{\hbar q}{m}|F|^2.

    Strictly speaking, the transmission coefficient is the ratio of probability currents, so

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            T &= \frac{J_\text{trans}}{J_\text{inc}}
            \\
            &= \frac{q}{k}\frac{|F|^2}{|A|^2} = \sqrt{\frac{E-V_0}{E}}\frac{|F|^2}{|A|^2}.
        \end{aligned}

    For `E<V_0`, `\psi=Fe^{-\kappa x}`, and

    .. math::
        \psi^*\psi' - \psi\psi'^* = 0,

    so `T=0`.

d.  From the boundary conditions in part (b),

    .. math::
        \frac{F}{A} = 1 + \frac{B}{A} = 1 + \frac{k-q}{k+q} = \frac{2k}{k+q},

    and therefore

    .. math::
        T = \frac{q}{k}\left|\frac{F}{A}\right|^2 = \frac{4kq}{(k+q)^2}.

    Indeed,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            R+T &= \left(\frac{k-q}{k+q}\right)^2 + \frac{4kq}{(k+q)^2}
            \\
            &= \frac{(k+q)^2}{(k+q)^2} = 1.
        \end{aligned}

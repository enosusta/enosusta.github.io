Problem 2.37
==================

From the normalization condition, changing the integration variable to `u=\pi x/a` gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        1 &= |A|^2\int_0^a\sin^6\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right)\,\dd x
        \\
        &= |A|^2\frac{a}{\pi}\int_0^\pi\sin^6(u)\,\dd u.
    \end{aligned}

Repeated application of the half-angle formulas gives

.. math::
    \sin^6(u) = \frac{10-15\cos(2u)+6\cos(4u)-\cos(6u)}{32},

so

.. math::
    1 = |A|^2\frac{5a}{16}.

Therefore, the normalization constant may be chosen as

.. math::
    A = \frac{4}{\sqrt{5a}}.

The initial state can be written as

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \Psi(x,0) &= \frac{A}{4}\left[3\sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right)-\sin\left(\frac{3\pi x}{a}\right)\right]
        \\
        &= \frac{3A}{4}\sqrt{\frac{a}{2}}\psi_1(x)-\frac{A}{4}\sqrt{\frac{a}{2}}\psi_3(x)
        \\
        &= \frac{3}{\sqrt{10}}\psi_1(x)-\frac{1}{\sqrt{10}}\psi_3(x),
    \end{aligned}

and hence

.. math::
    \Psi(x,t) = \frac{3}{\sqrt{10}}\psi_1(x)e^{-i E_1 t/\hbar}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{10}}\psi_3(x)e^{-i E_3 t/\hbar}.

For odd `n`,

.. math::
    \psi_n(a-x) = \psi_n(x),

so

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{x} &= \int_0^a x|\Psi(x,t)|^2\,\dd x
        \\
        &= \int_0^a (a-x)|\Psi(a-x,t)|^2\,\dd x
        \\
        &= a - \braket{x},
    \end{aligned}

which gives

.. math::
    \braket{x} = \frac{a}{2}.

Also,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{H} &= \frac{9}{10}E_1+\frac{1}{10}E_3
        \\
        &= \frac{9}{10}E_1 + \frac{9}{10}E_1
        \\
        &= \frac{9}{5}E_1 = \frac{9\pi^2\hbar^2}{10ma^2}.
    \end{aligned}

Problem 2.9
==================

Inside the well,

.. math::
    \hat{H} = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\dv{^2}{x^2}.

Since

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \dv{}{x}[Ax(a-x)] &= A(a-2x)
        \\
        \dv{^2}{x^2}[Ax(a-x)] &= -2A,
    \end{aligned}

we have

.. math::
    \hat{H}\Psi(x,0) = \frac{\hbar^2A}{m}.

Therefore,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{H} &= \int_0^a\Psi(x,0)^*\hat{H}\Psi(x,0)\,\dd x
        \\
        &= \frac{\hbar^2A^2}{m}\int_0^a (ax-x^2)\,\dd x
        \\
        &= \frac{\hbar^2 A^2}{m}\frac{a^3}{6} = \frac{5\hbar^2}{ma^2}.
    \end{aligned}

This agrees with the result obtained in Example 2.3.

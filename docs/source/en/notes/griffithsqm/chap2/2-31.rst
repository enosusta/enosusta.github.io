Problem 2.31
==================

Let the finite square-well potential be

.. math::
    V_a(x) = \begin{cases}
        -V_0 & (|x| \le a)
        \\
        0 & (|x| > a).
    \end{cases}

Its area is

.. math::
    \alpha \coloneqq \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} V_a(x) dx = -2 a V_0.

Holding `\alpha` fixed and taking `a\to 0`, for any smooth test function `f(x)` we have

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} V_a(x) f(x)\,\dd x &= -V_0 \int_{-a}^{a} f(x)\,\dd x
        \\
        &\to -V_0f(0)2a = -\alpha f(0),
    \end{aligned}

so

.. math::
    V_a(x) \to -\alpha \delta(x).

At the same time,

.. math::
    z_0 \coloneqq \frac{a}{\hbar}\sqrt{2mV_0} = \frac{\sqrt{m\alpha a}}{\hbar} \to 0.

Thus, although the depth tends to `V_0\to \infty`, the potential is “weak” in this sense.

Since `0 < la < z_0 \to 0`, the boundary condition becomes

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \kappa = l\tan(la) &\approx al^2
        \\
        &= a\left(\frac{2mV_0}{\hbar^2}-\kappa^2\right)
        \\
        &= \frac{m\alpha}{\hbar^2} - a\kappa^2 \to \frac{m\alpha}{\hbar^2}.
    \end{aligned}

Therefore, the bound-state energy is

.. math::
    E = -\frac{\hbar^2\kappa^2}{2m} \to -\frac{m\alpha^2}{2\hbar^2},

which agrees with the bound-state energy of the delta-function potential.

Meanwhile, Equation (2.172) becomes

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        T^{-1} -1 &\approx \frac{V_0^2}{4E(E+V_0)}(2al)^2
        \\
        &= \frac{V_0^2}{4E(E+V_0)}4a^2\frac{2m(E+V_0)}{\hbar^2}
        \\
        &= \frac{m\alpha^2}{2\hbar^2E},
    \end{aligned}

so

.. math::
    T \to \frac{1}{1 + m\alpha^2/(2\hbar^2E)},

which agrees with the transmission coefficient of the delta-function potential.

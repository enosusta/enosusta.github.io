Problem 2.16
==================

a.  Define `g(\xi)\coloneqq e^{-\xi^2}`. Differentiating gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            g' &= 2\xi e^{-\xi^2}
            \\
            g'' &= (4\xi^2-2)e^{-\xi^2}
            \\
            g''' &= (-8\xi^3+12\xi)e^{-\xi^2}
            \\
            g^{(4)} &= (16\xi^4-48\xi^2+12)e^{-\xi^2}.
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore, Rodrigues' formula `H_n=(-1)^n e^{\xi^2}g^{(n)}` gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            H_3(\xi) &= 8\xi^3-12\xi
            \\
            H_4(\xi) &= 16\xi^4-48\xi^2+12.
        \end{aligned}

b.  For `n=4`,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            H_5 &= 2\xi H_4-8H_3
            \\
            &= 2\xi(16\xi^4-48\xi^2+12)-8(8\xi^3-12\xi)
            \\
            &= 32\xi^5-160\xi^3+120\xi.
        \end{aligned}

    For `n=5`,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            H_6 &= 2\xi H_5-10H_4
            \\
            &= 2\xi(32\xi^5-160\xi^3+120\xi)-10(16\xi^4-48\xi^2+12)
            \\
            &= 64\xi^6-480\xi^4+720\xi^2-120.
        \end{aligned}

c.  From (b),

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            H_5' &= 160\xi^4-480\xi^2+120
            \\
            &= 10(16\xi^4-48\xi^2+12) = 2\cdot 5 H_4
            \\
            H_6' &= 384\xi^5-1920\xi^3+1440\xi
            \\
            &= 12(32\xi^5-160\xi^3+120\xi) = 2\cdot 6 H_5.
        \end{aligned}

    This verifies `H_n' = 2n H_{n-1}`.

d.  Expanding the two exponential functions through order `z^3` gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            e^{-z^2} &= 1-z^2+\mathcal{O}(z^4)
            \\
            e^{2z\xi} &= 1 + 2\xi z + \frac{(2\xi z)^2}{2!} + \frac{(2\xi z)^3}{3!} + \mathcal{O}(z^4)
            \\
            &= 1 + 2\xi z + 2\xi^2 z^2 + \frac{4\xi^3}{3} z^3 + \mathcal{O}(z^4).
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            e^{-z^2+2z\xi} &= (1-z^2)\left(1+2\xi z+2\xi^2 z^2 + \frac{4}{3}\xi^3 z^3\right) + \mathcal{O}(z^4)
            \\
            &= 1 + 2\xi z + (2\xi^2-1)z^2 + \left(\frac{4}{3}\xi^3-2\xi\right)z^3 + \mathcal{O}(z^4).
        \end{aligned}

    On the other hand, the right-hand side of the definition of the generating function is

    .. math::
        1 + H_1(\xi)z + \frac{H_2(\xi)}{2!}z^2 + \frac{H_3(\xi)}{3!}z^3 + \mathcal{O}(z^4).

    Comparing coefficients gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            H_1(\xi) &= 2\xi
            \\
            H_2(\xi) &= 4\xi^2-2
            \\
            H_3(\xi) &= 8\xi^3-12\xi.
        \end{aligned}

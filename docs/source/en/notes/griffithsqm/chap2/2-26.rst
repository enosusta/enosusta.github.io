Problem 2.26
==================

The Fourier transform of `\delta(x)` is

.. math::
    \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\delta(x)e^{-ikx}\,\dd x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}.

Taking the inverse Fourier transform gives

.. math::
    \delta(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{ikx}\,\dd k = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{ikx}\,\dd k.

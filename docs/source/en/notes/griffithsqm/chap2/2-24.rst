Problem 2.24
==================

Since `|\psi|^2` is an even function,

.. math::
    \braket{x} = 0.

Also,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{p} &= -i\hbar\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi\psi'\,\dd x
        \\
        &= -\frac{i\hbar}{2}[\psi^2]_{-\infty}^{\infty} = 0.
    \end{aligned}

Furthermore,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{x^2} &= 2\kappa\int_0^\infty x^2 e^{-2\kappa x}\,\dd x
        \\
        &= 2\kappa\frac{2}{(2\kappa)^3} = \frac{1}{2\kappa^2},
    \end{aligned}

so

.. math::
    \sigma_x = \sqrt{\braket{x^2} - \braket{x}^2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\kappa}.

Now, differentiating `|x|` gives

.. math::
    \dv{}{x}|x| = \theta(x)-\theta(-x),

and hence

.. math::
    \psi'(x) = -\kappa\sqrt{\kappa}[\theta(x)-\theta(-x)]e^{-\kappa|x|}.

Furthermore, from part (b) of :ref:`notes/griffithsqm/chap2/2-23:Problem 2.23`,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \psi''(x) &= -2\kappa\sqrt{\kappa}\delta(x)e^{-\kappa|x|} + \kappa^2\sqrt{\kappa}[\theta(x)-\theta(-x)]^2e^{-\kappa|x|}
        \\
        &= \kappa^2\psi(x) - 2\kappa\sqrt{\kappa}\delta(x),
    \end{aligned}

so

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \braket{p^2} &= -\hbar^2\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi(x)\psi''(x)\,\dd x
        \\
        &= -\hbar^2\kappa^2\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi^2(x)\,\dd x + 2\hbar^2\kappa\sqrt{\kappa}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi(x)\delta(x)\,\dd x
        \\
        &= -\hbar^2\kappa^2 + 2\hbar^2\kappa\sqrt{\kappa}\psi(0)
        \\
        &= -\hbar^2\kappa^2 + 2\hbar^2\kappa^2
        \\
        &= \hbar^2\kappa^2 = \left(\frac{m\alpha}{\hbar}\right)^2.
    \end{aligned}

Therefore,

.. math::
    \sigma_p = \sqrt{\braket{p^2} - \braket{p}^2} = \hbar\kappa,

and hence

.. math::
    \sigma_x\sigma_p = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\kappa}\hbar\kappa = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}} > \frac{\hbar}{2}.

Thus, the Heisenberg uncertainty principle is satisfied.

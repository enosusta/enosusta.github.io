Problem 2.54
==================

a.  From the scattering-matrix relations

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            B &= S_{11}A + S_{12}G
            \\
            F &= S_{21}A + S_{22}G
        \end{aligned}

    solving the first equation for `G` gives

    .. math::
        G = -\frac{S_{11}}{S_{12}}A + \frac{1}{S_{12}}B

    Substituting this into the second equation gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            F &= \left(S_{21}-\frac{S_{22}S_{11}}{S_{12}}\right)A + \frac{S_{22}}{S_{12}}B
            \\
            &= -\frac{\det\bm{S}}{S_{12}}A + \frac{S_{22}}{S_{12}}B
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \bm{M} = \frac{1}{S_{12}}\begin{pmatrix}
            -\det\bm{S} & S_{22}
            \\
            -S_{11} & 1
        \end{pmatrix}

    Conversely, solving the transfer-matrix relation

    .. math::
        G = M_{21}A + M_{22}B

    for `B` gives

    .. math::
        B = -\frac{M_{21}}{M_{22}}A + \frac{1}{M_{22}}G

    Substituting this into `F=M_{11}A+M_{12}B` gives

    .. math::
        F = \frac{\det\bm{M}}{M_{22}}A + \frac{M_{12}}{M_{22}}G

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \bm{S} = \frac{1}{M_{22}}\begin{pmatrix}
            -M_{21} & 1
            \\
            \det\bm{M} & M_{12}
        \end{pmatrix}

    Comparison with Equations (2.185) and (2.186) gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            R_l &= \left|\frac{M_{21}}{M_{22}}\right|^2,\quad T_l = \left|\frac{\det\bm{M}}{M_{22}}\right|^2
            \\
            R_r &= \left|\frac{M_{12}}{M_{22}}\right|^2,\quad T_r = \left|\frac{1}{M_{22}}\right|^2
        \end{aligned}

b.  Let the amplitudes to the left of the first potential, between the potentials, and to the right of the second potential be, respectively,

    .. math::
        \bm{v}_L = \begin{pmatrix}
            A
            \\
            B
        \end{pmatrix},\quad
        \bm{v}_C = \begin{pmatrix}
            C
            \\
            D
        \end{pmatrix},\quad
        \bm{v}_R = \begin{pmatrix}
            F
            \\
            G
        \end{pmatrix}

    By the definition of the transfer matrices,

    .. math::
        \bm{v}_C = \bm{M}_1\bm{v}_L,\quad
        \bm{v}_R = \bm{M}_2\bm{v}_C

    and hence

    .. math::
        \bm{v}_R = \bm{M}_2\bm{M}_1\bm{v}_L

    Therefore, the transfer matrix for the combined system is

    .. math::
        \bm{M} = \bm{M}_2\bm{M}_1

c.  To the left and right of the point `x=a`, let

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \psi_L &= A e^{ikx} + B e^{-ikx}
            \\
            \psi_R &= F e^{ikx} + G e^{-ikx}
        \end{aligned}

    The boundary conditions give

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            Fe^{ika} + Ge^{-ika} &= Ae^{ika} + Be^{-ika}
            \\
            ik(Fe^{ika}-Ge^{-ika}-Ae^{ika}+Be^{-ika}) &= -\frac{2m\alpha}{\hbar^2}(Ae^{ika}+Be^{-ika})
        \end{aligned}

    Defining `\beta\coloneqq m\alpha/\hbar^2 k` and solving for `F,G` gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            F &= (1+i\beta)A + i\beta e^{-2ika}B
            \\
            G &= -i\beta e^{2ika}A + (1-i\beta)B
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore, the transfer matrix is

    .. math::
        \bm{M}(a) = \begin{pmatrix}
            1+i\beta & i\beta e^{-2ika}
            \\
            -i\beta e^{2ika} & 1-i\beta
        \end{pmatrix}

    Its determinant is

    .. math::
        \det\bm{M}(a) = (1+i\beta)(1-i\beta)-\beta^2 = 1

d.  From part (b), the total transfer matrix is

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \bm{M} &= \bm{M}(a)\bm{M}(-a)
            \\
            &= \begin{pmatrix}
                (1+i\beta)^2+\beta^2e^{-4ika} & 2i\beta[\cos(2ka)-\beta\sin(2ka)]
                \\
                -2i\beta[\cos(2ka)-\beta\sin(2ka)] & (1-i\beta)^2 + \beta^2e^{4ika}
            \end{pmatrix}
        \end{aligned}

    For incidence from the left, each determinant is `1`, so `\det\bm{M}=1`, and

    .. math::
        T = \left|\frac{\det\bm{M}}{M_{22}}\right|^2 = \frac{1}{|M_{22}|^2}

    Since

    .. math::
        |M_{22}|^2 = 1 + 4\beta^2[\cos(2ka)-\beta\sin(2ka)]^2

    the transmission coefficient is

    .. math::
        T = \frac{1}{1 + 4\beta^2[\cos(2ka)-\beta\sin(2ka)]^2}

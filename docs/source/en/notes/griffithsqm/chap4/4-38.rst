Problem 4.38
==================

a.  Combining the spins of the first two quarks gives

    .. math::
        \frac{1}{2}\otimes\frac{1}{2}
        =
        1\oplus0.

    Adding the spin `1/2` of the third quark then gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            1\otimes\frac{1}{2}
            &=
            \frac{3}{2}\oplus\frac{1}{2},
            \\
            0\otimes\frac{1}{2}
            &=
            \frac{1}{2}.
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore, the possible total spins of a baryon are

    .. math::
        s=\frac{3}{2},
        \quad
        \frac{1}{2}.

    The `s=1/2` combination occurs in two independent ways.

b.  Both the quark and the antiquark have spin `1/2`, so

    .. math::
        \frac{1}{2}\otimes\frac{1}{2}
        =
        1\oplus0.

    Therefore, the possible total spins of a meson are

    .. math::
        s=1,
        \quad
        0.

Problem 4.21
==================

Since `L_x` and `L_y` are Hermitian operators,

.. math::
    L_+^\dagger
    =\left(L_x+iL_y\right)^\dagger
    =L_x-iL_y
    =L_-,
    \quad
    L_-^\dagger=L_+.

Since `f_l^m` is normalized, the norm of the state obtained by applying the raising operator is

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \left|A_l^m\right|^2
        &=
        \braket{L_+f_l^m|L_+f_l^m}
        \\
        &=
        \braket{f_l^m|L_-L_+|f_l^m}
        \\
        &=
        \braket{f_l^m|
        \left(L^2-L_z^2-\hbar L_z\right)
        |f_l^m}
        \\
        &=
        \hbar^2
        \left[l\left(l+1\right)-m\left(m+1\right)\right].
    \end{aligned}

Similarly, for the lowering operator,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \left|B_l^m\right|^2
        &=
        \braket{f_l^m|L_+L_-|f_l^m}
        \\
        &=
        \braket{f_l^m|
        \left(L^2-L_z^2+\hbar L_z\right)
        |f_l^m}
        \\
        &=
        \hbar^2
        \left[l\left(l+1\right)-m\left(m-1\right)\right].
    \end{aligned}

Thus, we may choose

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        A_l^m
        &=
        \hbar
        \sqrt{l\left(l+1\right)-m\left(m+1\right)}
        =
        \hbar\sqrt{\left(l-m\right)\left(l+m+1\right)},
        \\
        B_l^m
        &=
        \hbar
        \sqrt{l\left(l+1\right)-m\left(m-1\right)}
        =
        \hbar\sqrt{\left(l+m\right)\left(l-m+1\right)}.
    \end{aligned}

Therefore, at the two ends of the ladder,

.. math::
    A_l^l=0,
    \quad
    B_l^{-l}=0,
    \quad
    L_+f_l^l=0,
    \quad
    L_-f_l^{-l}=0.

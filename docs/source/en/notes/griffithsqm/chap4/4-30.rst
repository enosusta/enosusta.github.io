Problem 4.30
==================

a.  The normalization condition gives

    .. math::
        1
        =\chi^\dagger\chi
        =|A|^2\left(9+16\right)
        =25|A|^2,

    so we may take

    .. math::
        A=\frac{1}{5},
        \quad
        \chi=\frac{1}{5}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            3i\\
            4
        \end{pmatrix}.

b.  Using the spin matrices gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{S_x}
            &=
            \chi^\dagger S_x\chi
            =0,
            \\
            \braket{S_y}
            &=
            \chi^\dagger S_y\chi
            =-\frac{12}{25}\hbar,
            \\
            \braket{S_z}
            &=
            \chi^\dagger S_z\chi
            =-\frac{7}{50}\hbar.
        \end{aligned}

c.  For spin `1/2`,

    .. math::
        S_x^2=S_y^2=S_z^2
        =\frac{\hbar^2}{4},

    so `\braket{S_j^2}=\hbar^2/4`. Therefore,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \sigma_{S_x}
            &=
            \sqrt{\braket{S_x^2}-\braket{S_x}^2}
            =\frac{\hbar}{2},
            \\
            \sigma_{S_y}
            &=
            \sqrt{\frac{\hbar^2}{4}
            -\frac{144\hbar^2}{625}}
            =\frac{7\hbar}{50},
            \\
            \sigma_{S_z}
            &=
            \sqrt{\frac{\hbar^2}{4}
            -\frac{49\hbar^2}{2500}}
            =\frac{12\hbar}{25}.
        \end{aligned}

d.  Checking the three uncertainty relations in turn gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \sigma_{S_x}\sigma_{S_y}
            &=
            \frac{7\hbar^2}{100}
            =
            \frac{\hbar}{2}
            \left|\braket{S_z}\right|,
            \\
            \sigma_{S_y}\sigma_{S_z}
            &=
            \frac{42\hbar^2}{625}
            \geq
            \frac{\hbar}{2}
            \left|\braket{S_x}\right|
            =0,
            \\
            \sigma_{S_z}\sigma_{S_x}
            &=
            \frac{6\hbar^2}{25}
            =
            \frac{\hbar}{2}
            \left|\braket{S_y}\right|.
        \end{aligned}

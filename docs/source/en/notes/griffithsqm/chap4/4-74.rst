Problem 4.74
==================

a.  The scalar product of the magnetic field and spin is

    .. math::
        \bm{B}\cdot\bm{S}
        =
        -\alpha xS_x
        +\left(B_0+\alpha z\right)S_z.

    Using the matrix representations of `S_x` and `S_z`, the spin interaction becomes

    .. math::
        -\gamma\bm{B}\cdot\bm{S}
        =
        \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            -\left(B_0+\alpha z\right)&\alpha x\\
            \alpha x&B_0+\alpha z
        \end{pmatrix}.

    Therefore, the two components of the Schrödinger equation are

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\Psi_+
            &-
            \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}
            \left(B_0+\alpha z\right)\Psi_+
            +
            \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}\alpha x\Psi_-
            =
            i\hbar\pdv{\Psi_+}{t},
            \\
            -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\Psi_-
            &+
            \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}
            \left(B_0+\alpha z\right)\Psi_-
            +
            \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}\alpha x\Psi_+
            =
            i\hbar\pdv{\Psi_-}{t}.
        \end{aligned}

b.  Substituting `\Psi_\pm=e^{\pm i\gamma B_0t/2}\widetilde\Psi_\pm` into the equations in part (a), the diagonal terms due to the uniform field `B_0` cancel the terms arising from the time derivatives. The remaining equations are

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\widetilde\Psi_+
            &-
            \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}\alpha z\widetilde\Psi_+
            +
            \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}\alpha x
            e^{-i\gamma B_0t}\widetilde\Psi_-
            =
            i\hbar\pdv{\widetilde\Psi_+}{t},
            \\
            -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\widetilde\Psi_-
            &+
            \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}\alpha z\widetilde\Psi_-
            +
            \frac{\hbar\gamma}{2}\alpha x
            e^{i\gamma B_0t}\widetilde\Psi_+
            =
            i\hbar\pdv{\widetilde\Psi_-}{t}.
        \end{aligned}

c.  If the rapidly oscillating off-diagonal terms are averaged to zero, the two components decouple and move in the respective linear potentials

    .. math::
        V_+(z)=-\frac{\hbar\gamma\alpha}{2}z,
        \quad
        V_-(z)=\frac{\hbar\gamma\alpha}{2}z.

    The corresponding forces are

    .. math::
        F_z^{(+)}
        =-\dv{V_+}{z}
        =\frac{\hbar\gamma\alpha}{2},
        \quad
        F_z^{(-)}
        =-\dv{V_-}{z}
        =-\frac{\hbar\gamma\alpha}{2}.

    Thus, the spatial wave packets of the spin-up and spin-down components accelerate in opposite directions, splitting the incident beam into two. This agrees with `F_z=\gamma\alpha S_z`.

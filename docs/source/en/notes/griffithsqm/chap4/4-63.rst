Problem 4.63
==================

Write the spherical harmonics as

.. math::
    Y_l^m
    =
    K_l^m e^{im\phi}P_l^m\left(\cos\theta\right).

Applying the raising operator gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        L_+Y_l^m
        &=
        \hbar K_l^m e^{i\left(m+1\right)\phi}
        \left[
        \pdv{}{\theta}
        -m\cot\theta
        \right]
        P_l^m\left(\cos\theta\right)
        \\
        &=
        \hbar K_l^m e^{i\left(m+1\right)\phi}
        P_l^{m+1}\left(\cos\theta\right).
    \end{aligned}

In the second line, we used `x=\cos\theta` and the differentiation formula given in the problem. On the other hand, the raising operator acts on normalized angular-momentum eigenstates according to

.. math::
    L_+Y_l^m
    =
    \hbar\sqrt{\left(l-m\right)\left(l+m+1\right)}
    Y_l^{m+1}.

Comparing the two expressions gives

.. math::
    K_l^{m+1}
    =
    \frac{K_l^m}
    {\sqrt{\left(l-m\right)\left(l+m+1\right)}}.

Setting `C(l)\coloneqq K_l^0` and iterating this recurrence relation yields

.. math::
    K_l^m
    =
    C(l)
    \sqrt{\frac{\left(l-m\right)!}{\left(l+m\right)!}}.

Using the lowering operator extends this result to negative `m` as well.

Compare the result of :ref:`notes/griffithsqm/chap4/4-25:Problem 4.25` with

.. math::
    P_l^l\left(\cos\theta\right)
    =
    \frac{\left(-1\right)^l\left(2l\right)!}
    {2^l l!}
    \sin^l\theta.

The general expression above gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        Y_l^l
        &=
        \frac{C(l)}{\sqrt{\left(2l\right)!}}
        e^{il\phi}P_l^l\left(\cos\theta\right)
        \\
        &=
        \frac{\left(-1\right)^l C(l)\sqrt{\left(2l\right)!}}
        {2^l l!}
        \sin^l\theta e^{il\phi}.
    \end{aligned}

Equating its normalization factor to that in :ref:`notes/griffithsqm/chap4/4-25:Problem 4.25` gives

.. math::
    C(l)=\sqrt{\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}}.

Therefore, the required normalization factor is

.. math::
    K_l^m
    =
    \sqrt{
    \frac{2l+1}{4\pi}
    \frac{\left(l-m\right)!}{\left(l+m\right)!}
    }.

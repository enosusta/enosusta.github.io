Problem 4.39
==================

Read the Clebsch--Gordan table for `s_1=s_2=1/2`. For `m=1` and `m=-1`, there is only one nonzero coefficient in each case:

.. math::
    C_{\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{1}{2}\,1}
    ^{\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{1}{2}\,1}
    =1,
    \quad
    C_{-\frac{1}{2}\,-\frac{1}{2}\,-1}
    ^{\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{1}{2}\,1}
    =1.

Therefore,

.. math::
    \ket{1\,1}=\ket{\uparrow\uparrow},
    \quad
    \ket{1\,(-1)}=\ket{\downarrow\downarrow}.

The coefficients in the `m=0` sector are

.. math::
    \begin{array}{c|cc}
        (m_1,m_2)
        &s=1&s=0\\ \hline
        \left(\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\right)
        &\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\[2mm]
        \left(-\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)
        &\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
    \end{array}.

Thus, the expansion of the coupled states gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \ket{1\,0}
        &=
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \left(
        \ket{\uparrow\downarrow}
        +\ket{\downarrow\uparrow}
        \right),
        \\
        \ket{0\,0}
        &=
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \left(
        \ket{\uparrow\downarrow}
        -\ket{\downarrow\uparrow}
        \right).
    \end{aligned}

These agree with the triplet in Equation (4.175) and the singlet in Equation (4.176), respectively.

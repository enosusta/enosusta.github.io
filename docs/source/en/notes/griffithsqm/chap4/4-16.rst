Problem 4.16
==================

In the ground state,

.. math::
    |\psi_{100}(r,\theta,\phi)|^2
    =\frac{1}{\pi a^3}e^{-2r/a}.

The probability of finding the electron in a spherical shell between `r` and `r+\dd r` is

.. math::
    P(r)\,\dd r
    =4\pi r^2|\psi_{100}|^2\,\dd r
    =\frac{4r^2}{a^3}e^{-2r/a}\,\dd r.

Differentiating the radial probability density gives

.. math::
    \dv{P}{r}
    =\frac{8r}{a^3}e^{-2r/a}
    \left(1-\frac{r}{a}\right).

The stationary points are `r=0` and `r=a`. Since `P(0)=0` and `P(a)>0`, the maximum occurs at

.. math::
    r=a.

Therefore, the most probable radius is the Bohr radius.

Problem 4.56
==================

According to the Rydberg formula, the inverse wavelength for a transition `n_i\to n_f` is

.. math::
    \frac{1}{\lambda}
    =
    R
    \left(
    \frac{1}{n_f^2}-\frac{1}{n_i^2}
    \right).

Consider the two distinct pairs

.. math::
    \{n_i,n_f\}=\{7,5\},
    \quad
    \{n_i,n_f\}=\{35,7\}.

Then

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \frac{1}{5^2}-\frac{1}{7^2}
        &=
        \frac{49-25}{1225}
        =
        \frac{24}{1225},
        \\
        \frac{1}{7^2}-\frac{1}{35^2}
        &=
        \frac{25-1}{1225}
        =
        \frac{24}{1225}.
    \end{aligned}

Therefore, the transitions

.. math::
    7\longrightarrow5,
    \quad
    35\longrightarrow7

produce spectral lines with exactly the same wavelength.

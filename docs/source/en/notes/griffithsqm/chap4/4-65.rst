Problem 4.65
==================

Starting with the state of maximum total spin and successively applying `S_-=S_-^{(1)}+S_-^{(2)}+S_-^{(3)}`, the quartet is

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \ket{\frac{3}{2}\,\frac{3}{2}}
        &=\ket{\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow},
        \\
        \ket{\frac{3}{2}\,\frac{1}{2}}
        &=
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}
        \left(
        \ket{\downarrow\uparrow\uparrow}
        +\ket{\uparrow\downarrow\uparrow}
        +\ket{\uparrow\uparrow\downarrow}
        \right),
        \\
        \ket{\frac{3}{2}\,{-\frac{1}{2}}}
        &=
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}
        \left(
        \ket{\uparrow\downarrow\downarrow}
        +\ket{\downarrow\uparrow\downarrow}
        +\ket{\downarrow\downarrow\uparrow}
        \right),
        \\
        \ket{\frac{3}{2}\,{-\frac{3}{2}}}
        &=\ket{\downarrow\downarrow\downarrow}.
    \end{aligned}

Coupling the first two particles into a singlet gives the first doublet:

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \ket{\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{1}{2}}_1
        &=
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \left(
        \ket{\uparrow\downarrow\uparrow}
        -\ket{\downarrow\uparrow\uparrow}
        \right),
        \\
        \ket{\frac{1}{2}\,{-\frac{1}{2}}}_1
        &=
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \left(
        \ket{\uparrow\downarrow\downarrow}
        -\ket{\downarrow\uparrow\downarrow}
        \right).
    \end{aligned}

In the `m=1/2` subspace, choose a normalized state orthogonal to both the quartet and the first doublet, and construct its partner with the lowering operator. This gives the second doublet:

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \ket{\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{1}{2}}_2
        &=
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}
        \left(
        2\ket{\uparrow\uparrow\downarrow}
        -\ket{\uparrow\downarrow\uparrow}
        -\ket{\downarrow\uparrow\uparrow}
        \right),
        \\
        \ket{\frac{1}{2}\,{-\frac{1}{2}}}_2
        &=
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}
        \left(
        \ket{\uparrow\downarrow\downarrow}
        +\ket{\downarrow\uparrow\downarrow}
        -2\ket{\downarrow\downarrow\uparrow}
        \right).
    \end{aligned}

The states are orthonormal, and both doublets have `S^2=3\hbar^2/4`.

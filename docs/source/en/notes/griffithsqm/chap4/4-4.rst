Problem 4.4
==================

Rodrigues' formula gives

.. math::
    P_0(x)=1,
    \quad
    P_2(x)=\frac{1}{2}(3x^2-1),

so the associated Legendre functions are

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        P_0^0(x)&=1,
        \\
        P_2^1(x)
        &=-(1-x^2)^{1/2}\dv{}{x}P_2(x)
        \\
        &=-3x\sqrt{1-x^2}.
    \end{aligned}

Substituting these into the formula for the spherical harmonics gives

.. math::
    Y_0^0(\theta,\phi)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi}}

and

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        Y_2^1(\theta,\phi)
        &=\sqrt{
            \frac{5}{4\pi}\frac{(2-1)!}{(2+1)!}
        }e^{i\phi}P_2^1(\cos\theta)
        \\
        &=-\sqrt{\frac{15}{8\pi}}
        \sin\theta\cos\theta\,e^{i\phi}.
    \end{aligned}

First, for `Y_0^0`,

.. math::
    \int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi
    |Y_0^0|^2\sin\theta\,\dd\theta\,\dd\phi
    =\frac{1}{4\pi}(2\pi)(2)=1.

Also, using

.. math::
    \int_0^\pi\sin^3\theta\cos^2\theta\,\dd\theta
    =\frac{4}{15},

we find

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi
        |Y_2^1|^2\sin\theta\,\dd\theta\,\dd\phi
        &=\frac{15}{8\pi}(2\pi)
        \int_0^\pi\sin^3\theta\cos^2\theta\,\dd\theta
        \\
        &=1.
    \end{aligned}

Thus, both functions are normalized. Finally, since the inner product contains a single factor of `e^{i\phi}`,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi
        (Y_0^0)^*Y_2^1\sin\theta\,\dd\theta\,\dd\phi
        &\propto\int_0^{2\pi}e^{i\phi}\,\dd\phi
        \\
        &=0.
    \end{aligned}

Therefore, `Y_0^0` and `Y_2^1` are orthogonal.

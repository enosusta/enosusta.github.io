Problem 4.44
==================

Define the phase factor by

.. math::
    U\coloneqq e^{iq\Lambda/\hbar},
    \quad
    \Psi'=U\Psi.

Let the covariant momentum after the gauge transformation be

.. math::
    \bm{D}'
    \coloneqq
    -i\hbar\bm{\nabla}-q\bm{A}'.

Since `\bm{A}'=\bm{A}+\bm{\nabla}\Lambda`,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \bm{D}'\Psi'
        &=
        \left[
        -i\hbar\bm{\nabla}
        -q\left(\bm{A}+\bm{\nabla}\Lambda\right)
        \right]U\Psi
        \\
        &=
        U\left(
        -i\hbar\bm{\nabla}-q\bm{A}
        \right)\Psi.
    \end{aligned}

Applying this relation once more gives

.. math::
    \left(\bm{D}'\right)^2\Psi'
    =
    U
    \left(
    -i\hbar\bm{\nabla}-q\bm{A}
    \right)^2\Psi.

On the other hand, the time derivative is

.. math::
    i\hbar\pdv{\Psi'}{t}
    =
    U\left(
    i\hbar\pdv{\Psi}{t}
    -q\pdv{\Lambda}{t}\Psi
    \right).

Acting on `\Psi'` with the transformed Hamiltonian and using `\varphi'=\varphi-\partial\Lambda/\partial t` gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        \left[
        \frac{\left(\bm{D}'\right)^2}{2m}
        +q\varphi'
        \right]\Psi'
        &=
        U\left[
        \frac{
        \left(-i\hbar\bm{\nabla}-q\bm{A}\right)^2
        }{2m}
        +q\varphi
        -q\pdv{\Lambda}{t}
        \right]\Psi
        \\
        &=
        U\left(
        i\hbar\pdv{\Psi}{t}
        -q\pdv{\Lambda}{t}\Psi
        \right)
        \\
        &=
        i\hbar\pdv{\Psi'}{t}.
    \end{aligned}

Thus, `\Psi'` satisfies the Schrödinger equation for `\varphi'` and `\bm{A}'`. Also, since `|U|=1`, the probability density remains unchanged: `|\Psi'|^2=|\Psi|^2`.

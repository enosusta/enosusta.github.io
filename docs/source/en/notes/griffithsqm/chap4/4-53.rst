Problem 4.53
==================

a.  The radial function for `n=4` and `l=3`, and the spherical harmonic for `m=3`, are

    .. math::
        R_{43}(r)
        =
        \frac{1}{768\sqrt{35}a^{3/2}}
        \left(\frac{r}{a}\right)^3e^{-r/(4a)}

    and

    .. math::
        Y_3^3(\theta,\phi)
        =
        -\sqrt{\frac{35}{64\pi}}
        \sin^3\theta\,e^{3i\phi}.

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \psi_{433}(r,\theta,\phi)
        =
        -\frac{1}{6144\sqrt{\pi}a^{3/2}}
        \left(\frac{r}{a}\right)^3
        e^{-r/(4a)}
        \sin^3\theta\,e^{3i\phi}.

b.  The radial dependence of `|R_{43}|^2r^2` is proportional to `r^8e^{-r/(2a)}`. Thus, without writing the normalization constant explicitly,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \braket{r}
            &=
            \frac{
            \displaystyle
            \int_0^\infty r^9e^{-r/(2a)}\,\dd r
            }{
            \displaystyle
            \int_0^\infty r^8e^{-r/(2a)}\,\dd r
            }
            \\
            &=
            \frac{9!\left(2a\right)^{10}}
            {8!\left(2a\right)^9}
            =18a.
        \end{aligned}

c.  We have `L_x^2+L_y^2=L^2-L_z^2`. Since `\psi_{433}` is a simultaneous eigenstate of `L^2` and `L_z`,

    .. math::
        \left(L_x^2+L_y^2\right)\psi_{433}
        =
        \hbar^2
        \left[
        l\left(l+1\right)-m^2
        \right]\psi_{433}
        =
        3\hbar^2\psi_{433}.

    Therefore, the only possible measurement result is `3\hbar^2`, and its probability is one.

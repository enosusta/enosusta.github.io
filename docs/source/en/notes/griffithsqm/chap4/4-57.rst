Problem 4.57
==================

a.  Since `L_z=xp_y-yp_x`,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            [x^2,L_z]
            &=
            [x^2,xp_y]-[x^2,yp_x]
            \\
            &=
            -y[x^2,p_x]
            =
            -2i\hbar xy.
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore, the uncertainty principle gives

    .. math::
        \sigma_{x^2}\sigma_{L_z}
        \geq
        \frac{1}{2}
        \left|\braket{[x^2,L_z]}\right|
        =
        \hbar|\braket{xy}|.

b.  A hydrogen state `\psi_{nlm}` is an eigenstate of `L_z`, with

    .. math::
        L_z\psi_{nlm}
        =
        m\hbar\psi_{nlm}.

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \sigma_{L_z}
        =
        \sqrt{\braket{L_z^2}-\braket{L_z}^2}
        =0.

c.  Substituting part (b) into the uncertainty relation from part (a) gives

    .. math::
        0
        \geq
        \hbar|\braket{xy}|.

    Since the right-hand side is nonnegative, we must have

    .. math::
        \braket{xy}=0.

Problem 4.58
==================

a.  The normalization condition gives

    .. math::
        1
        =
        |A|^2
        \left(
        |1-2i|^2+|2|^2
        \right)
        =
        9|A|^2.

    Choosing the overall phase to be zero, we obtain

    .. math::
        A=\frac{1}{3},
        \quad
        \chi=
        \frac{1}{3}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            1-2i\\
            2
        \end{pmatrix}.

b.  The eigenvalues of `S_z` are `\pm\hbar/2`. The probabilities are the squared magnitudes of the corresponding spinor components, so

    .. math::
        P_z^+
        =
        \frac{5}{9},
        \quad
        P_z^-
        =
        \frac{4}{9}.

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \braket{S_z}
        =
        \frac{\hbar}{2}
        \left(
        P_z^+-P_z^-
        \right)
        =
        \frac{\hbar}{18}.

c.  The normalized eigenspinors of `S_x` are

    .. math::
        \chi_\pm^{(x)}
        =
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            1\\
            \pm1
        \end{pmatrix}.

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            P_x^+
            &=
            \frac{1}{2}
            \left|
            \frac{1-2i}{3}+\frac{2}{3}
            \right|^2
            =
            \frac{13}{18},
            \\
            P_x^-
            &=
            \frac{1}{2}
            \left|
            \frac{1-2i}{3}-\frac{2}{3}
            \right|^2
            =
            \frac{5}{18}.
        \end{aligned}

    The possible measurement results are `\pm\hbar/2`, and

    .. math::
        \braket{S_x}
        =
        \frac{\hbar}{2}
        \left(
        P_x^+-P_x^-
        \right)
        =
        \frac{2\hbar}{9}.

d.  The normalized eigenspinors of `S_y` are

    .. math::
        \chi_+^{(y)}
        =
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            1\\
            i
        \end{pmatrix},
        \quad
        \chi_-^{(y)}
        =
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            1\\
            -i
        \end{pmatrix}.

    Hence,

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            P_y^+
            &=
            \frac{1}{2}
            \left|
            \frac{1-2i}{3}
            -i\frac{2}{3}
            \right|^2
            =
            \frac{17}{18},
            \\
            P_y^-
            &=
            \frac{1}{2}
            \left|
            \frac{1-2i}{3}
            +i\frac{2}{3}
            \right|^2
            =
            \frac{1}{18}.
        \end{aligned}

    The possible measurement results are `\pm\hbar/2`, and

    .. math::
        \braket{S_y}
        =
        \frac{\hbar}{2}
        \left(
        P_y^+-P_y^-
        \right)
        =
        \frac{4\hbar}{9}.

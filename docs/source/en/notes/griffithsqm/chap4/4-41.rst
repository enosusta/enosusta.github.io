Problem 4.41
==================

Expand the square of the total angular momentum as

.. math::
    S^2
    =
    \left(\bm{S}^{(1)}+\bm{S}^{(2)}\right)^2
    =
    \left(S^{(1)}\right)^2
    +\left(S^{(2)}\right)^2
    +2\bm{S}^{(1)}\cdot\bm{S}^{(2)}.

Operators belonging to different particles commute with one another, and `(S^{(1)})^2` also commutes with `S_z^{(1)}`. Therefore,

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        [S^2,S_z^{(1)}]
        &=
        2\left[
        S_x^{(1)}S_x^{(2)}
        +S_y^{(1)}S_y^{(2)},
        S_z^{(1)}
        \right]
        \\
        &=
        2\left(
        [S_x^{(1)},S_z^{(1)}]S_x^{(2)}
        +[S_y^{(1)},S_z^{(1)}]S_y^{(2)}
        \right)
        \\
        &=
        2i\hbar
        \left(
        S_x^{(1)}S_y^{(2)}
        -S_y^{(1)}S_x^{(2)}
        \right)
        \\
        &=
        2i\hbar
        \left(\bm{S}^{(1)}\times\bm{S}^{(2)}\right)_z.
    \end{aligned}

Carrying out the same calculation for an arbitrary component `i` gives

.. math::
    \begin{aligned}
        [S^2,S_i^{(1)}]
        &=
        2\sum_j
        [S_j^{(1)},S_i^{(1)}]S_j^{(2)}
        \\
        &=
        2i\hbar
        \left(\bm{S}^{(1)}\times\bm{S}^{(2)}\right)_i.
    \end{aligned}

Thus, in vector form,

.. math::
    [S^2,\bm{S}^{(1)}]
    =
    2i\hbar
    \left(\bm{S}^{(1)}\times\bm{S}^{(2)}\right).

Similarly,

.. math::
    [S^2,\bm{S}^{(2)}]
    =
    -2i\hbar
    \left(\bm{S}^{(1)}\times\bm{S}^{(2)}\right).

Adding these two equations also verifies `[S^2,\bm{S}]=0`.

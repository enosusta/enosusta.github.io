Problem 4.32
==================

a.  Let `\lambda` be an eigenvalue of `S_y`. Since

    .. math::
        \det\left(
        \frac{\hbar}{2}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            0&-i\\
            i&0
        \end{pmatrix}
        -\lambda\mathsf{I}
        \right)
        =
        \lambda^2-\frac{\hbar^2}{4}
        =0,

    the eigenvalues are `\lambda_\pm=\pm\hbar/2`. The corresponding normalized eigenspinors may be chosen as

    .. math::
        \chi_+^{(y)}
        =
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            1\\
            i
        \end{pmatrix},
        \quad
        \chi_-^{(y)}
        =
        \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
        \begin{pmatrix}
            1\\
            -i
        \end{pmatrix}.

b.  The inner products of a general state `\chi=(a,b)^{\mathsf T}` with the two eigenspinors are

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \left(\chi_+^{(y)}\right)^\dagger\chi
            &=
            \frac{a-ib}{\sqrt{2}},
            \\
            \left(\chi_-^{(y)}\right)^\dagger\chi
            &=
            \frac{a+ib}{\sqrt{2}}.
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore, the possible measurement results and their probabilities are

    .. math::
        \begin{array}{c|c}
            S_y & P\\ \hline
            +\hbar/2 & \dfrac{1}{2}|a-ib|^2\\[2mm]
            -\hbar/2 & \dfrac{1}{2}|a+ib|^2
        \end{array}.

    Even when `a` and `b` are complex,

    .. math::
        \frac{1}{2}|a-ib|^2
        +\frac{1}{2}|a+ib|^2
        =
        |a|^2+|b|^2
        =1,

    so the probabilities sum to one.

c.  Since `S_y^2=\hbar^2/4`, for every normalized state,

    .. math::
        S_y^2\chi=\frac{\hbar^2}{4}\chi.

    Thus, the only possible measurement result is `\hbar^2/4`, and its probability is one.

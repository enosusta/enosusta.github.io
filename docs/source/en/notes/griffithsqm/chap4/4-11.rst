Problem 4.11
==================

For a bound state, `-V_0<E<0`. Define

.. math::
    k\coloneqq\frac{\sqrt{2m(E+V_0)}}{\hbar},
    \quad
    \kappa\coloneqq\frac{\sqrt{-2mE}}{\hbar}.

Then

.. math::
    k^2+\kappa^2=\frac{2mV_0}{\hbar^2}.

For `l=0`, the radial equation inside and outside the well is, respectively,

.. math::
    \dv{^2u}{r^2}
    =
    \begin{cases}
        -k^2u,&0<r\leq a,\\
        \kappa^2u,&r>a.
    \end{cases}

Choosing a solution that satisfies `u(0)=0` at the origin and decays at infinity gives

.. math::
    u(r)=
    \begin{cases}
        A\sin(kr),&0\leq r\leq a,\\
        Be^{-\kappa r},&r>a.
    \end{cases}

Continuity of `u` and `u'` at `r=a` gives

.. math::
    A\sin(ka)=Be^{-\kappa a},
    \quad
    Ak\cos(ka)=-B\kappa e^{-\kappa a}.

Taking the ratio of these two equations yields the eigenvalue condition

.. math::
    k\cot(ka)=-\kappa.

Introduce the dimensionless quantities

.. math::
    x\coloneqq ka,
    \quad
    y\coloneqq\kappa a,
    \quad
    z_0\coloneqq\frac{a}{\hbar}\sqrt{2mV_0}.

Then

.. math::
    x^2+y^2=z_0^2,
    \quad
    y=-x\cot x.

Therefore, the smallest solution `x_1`, which corresponds to the ground state, is determined by

.. math::
    \sqrt{z_0^2-x_1^2}=-x_1\cot x_1,
    \quad
    \frac{\pi}{2}<x_1<\min(\pi,z_0).

The corresponding energy is

.. math::
    E_0
    =-V_0+\frac{\hbar^2x_1^2}{2ma^2}
    =-\frac{\hbar^2}{2ma^2}\left(z_0^2-x_1^2\right).

Also, since

.. math::
    B=A\sin x_1\,e^{y_1},
    \quad
    y_1=\sqrt{z_0^2-x_1^2},

the normalized ground state can be written as

.. math::
    \psi_0(r,\theta,\phi)
    =\frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi}}\frac{u_0(r)}{r},

where

.. math::
    u_0(r)=A
    \begin{cases}
        \sin(x_1r/a),&0\leq r\leq a,\\
        \sin x_1\,e^{-y_1(r/a-1)},&r>a.
    \end{cases}

The normalization constant is given by

.. math::
    |A|^{-2}
    =
    \frac{a}{2}
    -\frac{a\sin(2x_1)}{4x_1}
    +\frac{a\sin^2x_1}{2y_1}.

Finally, since `y>0`, the ground-state solution of the eigenvalue condition `y=-x\cot x` requires `x>\pi/2`. On the other hand, we must have `x<z_0`. Thus, a bound state can exist only if

.. math::
    z_0>\frac{\pi}{2}.

Equivalently, we must have

.. math::
    V_0a^2>\frac{\pi^2\hbar^2}{8m},

and no bound state exists if

.. math::
    V_0a^2<\frac{\pi^2\hbar^2}{8m}.

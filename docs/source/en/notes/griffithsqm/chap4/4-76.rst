Problem 4.76
==================

a.  Faraday's law of induction gives the electromotive force around the ring as

    .. math::
        \mathcal{E}
        =
        \oint\bm{E}\cdot\dd\bm{l}
        =
        2\pi bE_\phi
        =-\dv{\Phi}{t}.

    If the tangential velocity of the particle is `v_\phi=b\omega`, the rate at which the electric field does work on the particle is

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \dv{W}{t}
            &=q\bm{E}\cdot\bm{v}
            =qE_\phi b\omega
            \\
            &=-\frac{q\omega}{2\pi}\dv{\Phi}{t}.
        \end{aligned}

    Therefore,

    .. math::
        \dv{W}{\Phi}
        =-q\frac{\omega}{2\pi}.

b.  On the ring, the vector potential is `\bm{A}=\left[\Phi/(2\pi b)\right]\hat{\bm{\phi}}`. Therefore, the `z` component of the mechanical angular momentum is

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            L_{\mathrm{mechanical},z}
            &=
            \left[
            \bm{r}\times
            \left(\bm{p}-q\bm{A}\right)
            \right]_z
            \\
            &=
            -i\hbar\pdv{}{\phi}
            -\frac{q\Phi}{2\pi}.
        \end{aligned}

    Acting on the state `\psi_n=e^{in\phi}/\sqrt{2\pi}` gives

    .. math::
        L_{\mathrm{mechanical},z}\psi_n
        =
        \left(
        n\hbar-\frac{q\Phi}{2\pi}
        \right)\psi_n.

    Hence,

    .. math::
        mb^2\omega
        =
        n\hbar-\frac{q\Phi}{2\pi},

    which is not, in general, an integer multiple of `\hbar`.

c.  Write the energy of the stationary state as

    .. math::
        E_n
        =
        \frac{1}{2mb^2}
        \left(
        n\hbar-\frac{q\Phi}{2\pi}
        \right)^2.

    Differentiating with respect to the magnetic flux and using the angular velocity from part (b) gives

    .. math::
        \begin{aligned}
            \dv{E_n}{\Phi}
            &=
            -\frac{q}{2\pi mb^2}
            \left(
            n\hbar-\frac{q\Phi}{2\pi}
            \right)
            \\
            &=-q\frac{\omega}{2\pi}
            =\dv{W}{\Phi}.
        \end{aligned}

    Thus, the change in the quantum-mechanical energy level agrees exactly with the work done classically by electromagnetic induction.

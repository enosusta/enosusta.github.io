Solutions to Griffiths's QM (3rd Ed.)
========================================

Practice is essential for learning how to use quantum mechanics. Fortunately, the excellent textbook :cite:p:`griffiths2018` contains both basic, straightforward exercises and extremely challenging problems in every chapter and section. Professional instructors can apparently obtain the official solutions manual from the publisher, but students do not have access to it. The following are solutions that I prepared. I cannot guarantee that they are correct.
